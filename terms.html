Terms & Conditions

These terms apply to lead research and prospect list services provided by Alcode. Each project begins with a written quote confirming its scope, deliverables, fees, and expected schedule.

Last updated November 1, 2026

1. Agreement and project quote

These terms and the project-specific written quote together describe the service arrangement. Approval of a quote or written instruction to begin work confirms the agreed project. If a quote sets a different project-specific scope, fee, or schedule, the quote controls for that item.

2. Services and scope

Alcode provides the research, prospect data, and related deliverables listed in the accepted quote. The quote will describe the target audience, relevant criteria, list size or volume, research depth, and delivery format where applicable. Changes to the brief or requested deliverables may affect fees and timing. We will discuss and receive your approval for a revised scope before starting additional work.

3. Custom pricing and payment

Pricing is customized to the project and stated in its written quote. Payment amounts, due dates, and any project-specific payment stages will be set out in that quote or its invoice. Work outside the accepted scope will be quoted for approval before it begins.

4. Client information and collaboration

You agree to provide clear audience criteria, accurate project details, and timely feedback or approvals needed to complete the work. We will ask questions early, keep progress visible, and work with you to keep the project aligned with your goals. Delays in required information or approvals may require an updated delivery schedule, which we will communicate promptly.

5. Confidentiality

Alcode treats non-public client information as confidential. This includes business information, briefs, audience criteria, campaign plans, and materials shared for a project. We use this information only to perform the agreed services and limit access to people who need it for that work. We will not disclose or reuse identifiable client materials without your prior written permission, except when disclosure is required by law.

6. Research and data quality

We research to the agreed criteria, check records for relevance, and prioritize current, active contact data. Our process uses established sales principles to focus outreach on prospects aligned with your offer. We value our partners and work toward their long-term success by using feedback to keep research focused on their business goals.

7. Communication and delivery

The expected delivery schedule and update cadence will be agreed for each project. We aim to respond to inquiries as soon as possible, usually within one business day. We will keep you informed of progress and promptly communicate any issue that may affect an agreed timeline.

8. Responsible use of research

You are responsible for ensuring that your use of delivered data and your outreach comply with the laws, platform rules, and consent requirements applicable to your organization and recipients. Alcode provides research services; it does not send outreach on your behalf unless this is expressly included in a separate written agreement.

9. Project changes and questions

Requests to change or pause a project should be made in writing. Any resulting schedule or fee changes will be discussed with you before the revised work proceeds. If a delivered item appears inconsistent with the agreed criteria, contact us promptly so we can review it together and agree on a practical next step.

10. Contact

For a project quote or questions about these terms, use the contact form and we will respond as soon as possible.
